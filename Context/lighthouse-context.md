# Lighthouse Sanctuary — INTEX Project Context

## The Real Organization

**Lighthouse Sanctuary** (Filipino: *Paro de Santuario*) is a non-profit safe house organization operating in the Philippines, founded by Julie and her husband. It serves girls who have been trafficked, sexually abused, or subjected to online sexual exploitation.

- Has served **150+ survivors** across two shelters
- Operates in **Region 1 of the Philippines** (first safe house ever in that region)
- Has a second shelter **7 hours from the first**, funded by LDS Charities
- Provides holistic care: housing, food, education, church attendance, and spiritual development
- Mission rooted in gospel healing: replacing despair with hope through Christ's atonement

### Why It Matters — Philippines Context (UNICEF data)
- 1 in 5 children have experienced sexual abuse
- 33% of abuse cases involve incest
- 1 in 5 teenagers (ages 12–18) have been subjected to online sexual exploitation
- The Philippines is considered a **global hotspot** for online child sexual abuse
- COVID-19 increased online sexual abuse cases by **265%** (2021 alone)

### Organization Motto (written by the girls themselves)
> "We are Lighthouse. Full of hope, love, and new beginnings. Our focus is progress in all aspects of life. We treat each other as family, where each individual is seen, heard, and loved. We create fun memories. We fight for justice. And we acknowledge God in all we do."

### Future Vision
- Place one safe house in **every region of the Philippines** (20+ regions, aligned with LDS missions)
- Continue empowering survivors as advocates and voices against abuse

## The INTEX Project

### Important: You Are NOT Building for Lighthouse Directly
The dataset and scenario are **inspired by Lighthouse but anonymized and synthetic**. You will:
- Create a **fictional 503(c) nonprofit organization** (you choose the name)
- Model it as an organization replicating Lighthouse's work **somewhere else in the world**
- Present to "judges" who roleplay as this fictional organization seeking to launch

### Core Problem to Solve
Build a web application that helps this fictional organization:

1. **Protect and track the girls** — educational, physical, and social development goals
2. **Donor management** — attract, retain, and expand donor base; track donation history
3. **Social media strategy** — tools or tracking for donor-facing communications
4. **Admin functionality** — admin users who can add/edit records; role-based access

### Data
- **17 database tables** provided, normalized, with built-in correlations
- Includes a **15-page data dictionary** at the bottom of the case document
- Use AI to help summarize — it's more data than most class projects
- Should be importable into a real relational database
